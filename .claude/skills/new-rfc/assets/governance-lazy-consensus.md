## How decisions are made

The project decides by lazy consensus. A maintainer states a proposal in the open. If no maintainer objects within the review period, the proposal is accepted. Silence counts as agreement.

When a maintainer objects, the maintainers discuss it. If they do not reach agreement, they hold a vote. Each maintainer has one vote, and a simple majority decides.

## Escalation

When a vote ends without a majority, the proposal does not pass. Any maintainer can bring a revised proposal.

## Changing this document

A change to the governance model needs a two-thirds majority of the maintainers. Make the change through the strongest route the repository has. Where the repository has an RFC process, use an RFC. Otherwise, record an owner decision. The maintainers adopt this document through their normal review.

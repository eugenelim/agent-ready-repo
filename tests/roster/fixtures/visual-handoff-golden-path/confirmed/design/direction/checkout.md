# Aesthetic direction: Checkout

Fixture data for a roster test. No person or product is described.

## Named goals (ranked)

1. **Calm assurance** — means: the buyer never wonders whether payment worked.
2. **Clear next step** — means: one obvious action per screen.
3. **Familiar brand** — means: the existing brand palette carries over unchanged where it can.

**Primary action or continuation:** pay for the order.

## Approved visual target

**Target:** a two-region checkout composition, the payment form leading and the order summary trailing.

**Binding:** composition, proportion and the order of the two regions.

**Illustrative:** the sketch's colours and type, which bind nothing.

**May adapt responsively:** the regions stack at the narrow channel, form first.

**Confirmation record:** 2026-09-30 — the checkout design review notes, recorded here as `visual_target: confirmed`.

# Analyse the market

Ask for the analysis your decision needs, and your agent writes it up as a document your team can read. Each one answers a different question, so run only the ones you need.

## Your position: SWOT

To see your strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats, ask:

```text
Run a SWOT for the payments product.
```

The agent writes `docs/product/shaping/swot-analysis.md`. Each entry should be a specific claim with a consequence, such as "brand recognition lets us charge 15% more", not a single word.

## The market's pressure: Porter's Five Forces

To see how hard the market's structure pushes on you, ask:

```text
Run Porter's Five Forces on the market we're entering.
```

The agent rates suppliers, buyers, new entrants, substitutes, and rivalry, and says why each is high or low. It writes `competitive-landscape.md`.

## Forces outside the market: PESTLE

To see political, economic, social, technological, legal, and environmental forces, ask:

```text
Run a PESTLE scan for the regulated-markets expansion.
```

The agent writes `macro-environment.md`, with the forces that move your decision weighted most.

## Your product line: BCG matrix

To place several products by market growth and share, ask:

```text
Place our product line on a BCG matrix.
```

The agent writes `portfolio-position.md`. It needs more than one product to be useful.

## Run more than one

A common order is PESTLE and Five Forces first, then SWOT to bring them together, then BCG if you are deciding across products. Each document stands on its own and points to the others instead of repeating them.

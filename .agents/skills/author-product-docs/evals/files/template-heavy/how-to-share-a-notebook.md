# How to share a notebook

<!-- DRAFT: tighten the intro before review -->
Diátaxis kind: how-to guide

Share a notebook so a teammate can read and edit it.

<!-- rung: 3a — prerequisites -->
## Before you start

You need a Fieldlog account and edit rights on the notebook. <!-- TODO: link to the rights page -->

<!-- rung: 3b — steps -->
## Steps

1. Open the notebook and choose **Share**.
2. Enter your teammate's email address.
3. Pick **Can edit** or **Can view**.
4. Choose **Send invite**.

<!-- rung: 3c — result -->
## Result

Your teammate gets an email with a link. The notebook shows their name under **People**.

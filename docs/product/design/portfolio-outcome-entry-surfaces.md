# Portfolio outcome entry surfaces

- **Status:** Draft

## Outcome

Homepage, catalogue, pack, README, journey, and documentation entry surfaces
route adopters by plain-language outcome while preserving each surface's
existing owner and renderer contract.

## Design question

Define the cross-surface information architecture that consumes the shipped
portfolio first-value contract and pilot evidence without duplicating the
tech-site or design-system programmes.

## Constraints

- Preserve the separate owners of site chrome, responsiveness, and the site
  design system.
- Treat the portfolio contract and three completed pilots as design inputs.
- Produce a surface/flow brief before proposing renderer changes.

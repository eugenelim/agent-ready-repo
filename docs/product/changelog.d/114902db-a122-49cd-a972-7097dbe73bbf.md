## Highlights
- See what an intent waits for and what waits for it, in the query, its text output and the offline view.
- Pick an intent in the offline view and its Dependencies part lists "A waits for B" lines, with Show and Steps controls; dependency problems also appear in "What is broken?".

## Changed
- `navigate-intents` gains a `dependencies` query operation (`--id`, `--direction prerequisites|dependents|both`, `--depth`, `--format text`) over intents' `Depends on:` headers. It accepts typed references and legacy repository paths, lists values it cannot resolve as diagnostics, reads no workspace dependencies and decides no readiness.
- The offline view's data carries a `dependencies` member, and a `--from` export also carries the dependency records of intents linked to its subtree and names those outside it by id.

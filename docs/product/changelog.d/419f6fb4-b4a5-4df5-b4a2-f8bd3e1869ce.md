## Highlights
- `repo-governance` 2.5.3 gives adopters a six-section RFC template, a standard-library `rfc.py` script that numbers and checks RFCs, a starter `docs/GOVERNANCE.md` and a contributor issue template.
- The `new-rfc` skill numbers drafts with the bundled `rfc.py` and asks who decides instead of naming an approver.

## Changed
- Seeded files reach new installs only, and `agentbundle upgrade` neither refreshes nor adds them. A fix to a seeded script such as `rfc.py` reaches an existing adopter only by re-copying the script.
- `agentbundle` 0.60.2 makes `catalogue self-host` keep `docs/GOVERNANCE.md` repository-owned and teaches the catalogue lint the new `repo-governance` seed shapes.

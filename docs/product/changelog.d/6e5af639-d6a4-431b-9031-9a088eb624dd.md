## Highlights
- Ask which record a decision needs, and navigate-decisions now routes it read-only, with a door verdict and who to consult.

## Changed
- `navigate-decisions` gains a read-only `route` mode that picks the record a decision needs, gives an advisory one-way or two-way door verdict, and lists the handles and roles to consult. It writes nothing.
- `new-adr` `Consulted` now takes handles and roles only, and the ADR lint flags hostile characters.

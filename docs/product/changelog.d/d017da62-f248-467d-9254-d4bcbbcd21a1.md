## Highlights
- A delivery brief can carry an optional run grant, `docs/product/run-grants/<brief-slug>.toml`, set once at the Ready stop: when it expires (at most 14 days), when the run counts as done, which slices may run, the retry budget, one slice at a time, and extra actions the human keeps. A grant only narrows what the brief-level runner would do and carries no authority: approvals, merges and the other human-only actions stay human.
- The brief-level runner binds a grant to the brief and standing orders it was confirmed with, and answers `grant-refused` for a missing, stale or malformed grant and `grant-spent` once a limit is spent, while still listing pending gates and merge-ready slices. Its `next` answer moves to schema version 2.
- The runner starts no slice from a brief that is not `Ready` or `Executing`; slices already running from such a brief may resume until 2026-11-30.
- A new read-only `brief-runner.py guard` verb fails a change that mixes a grant change with other work; it protects only where a repository runs the base revision's copy as a required check.
- `author-delivery-brief continue` offers a grant path that asks Ready, the slice cut and the grant as three separate answers at one stop, and shows every limit a grant cannot bound. Spend is not bounded.

## Highlights
- The work-loop supervisor's contracts now describe concurrent attempts: new access-attestation, launcher-message, result-integration, and integration-conflict records, a `run-concurrent` request kind, and extended containment-attestation, execution-result, and task-contract records.
- A plan task may declare `Reads:` and `Writes:` paths; both count toward the task's revision, and a malformed or protected entry is recorded as a reason the task stays on the sequential floor.
- `supervisor-policy.json` gains `concurrency` (shipped `sequential`), `max_concurrent_attempts`, `reprojection_bound`, and `attempt_timeout_seconds`, each range-checked at `start`. Nothing runs concurrently yet: the shipped policy keeps every task on the one-at-a-time floor.

## Highlights
- The review classifier now names the rule a report fails when its numbered findings carry no `path:line` anchor: `review-artifact.py raw-classify` and flagless `inspect` refuse it as `finding-unanchored` instead of reporting a missing clean sentence. The classification each report gets is unchanged.
- `adversarial-reviewer` states the anchor rule — a backticked repository-relative `path:line` right after each finding's bold title, and a finding about something absent cites the line where it belongs — with a concrete finding line that parses. Its typed report, like `quality-engineer`'s, puts that anchor first in `evidence_refs`.

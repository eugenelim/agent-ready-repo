## Highlights
- The work-loop traceability check now reads an intent's `Kind:`, `Level:` and `Slug:` only from its header block above the first `## ` heading, and ignores commented-out lines. It places each intent on the outcome, opportunity and capability ladder exactly as the intent navigator shows it.
- A retired (tombstoned) intent no longer appears as a node on the ladder.
- An intent whose `Kind:` sits only in its body, in a comment, or on a lowercase `**kind:**` label no longer counts as a ladder rung. Move the `Kind:` line into the header block to keep it on the ladder.

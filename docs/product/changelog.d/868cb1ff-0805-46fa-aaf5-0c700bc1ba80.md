## Highlights
- Repo-origin intents and delivery briefs are no longer registered in workspace.toml: an intent or brief is its own file, and its Status: line is its lifecycle.
- A new workspace-status repair step, intent-brief-drop, removes existing repo-origin intent and brief entries; tracker-origin entries and captured items keep theirs.
- The intent rename works without a registry entry, and an archived spec's parent brief and a brief's child-state check resolve from the brief's own file.

## Changed
- `work-intake`: the start, remember, and ready-brief routes for a repo-origin intent or brief return no membership and register nothing; the intent rename fixes its registry mode from the base commit, so an unregistered intent renames without touching `workspace.toml`.
- `workspace-status`: the `intent-brief-drop` repair step; a cooled spec whose declared parent is a listed brief file is attributed to it; a dependency on an unregistered brief whose `Status:` contradicts its child specs is refused.
- `author-delivery-brief`, `close-work`, and `intake-intent` register or move only a tracker-origin brief or intent entry, and send a leftover repo-origin entry to the repair.
- `work-loop`: the spec-status lint resolves a deferral anchor through an open intent or brief file.

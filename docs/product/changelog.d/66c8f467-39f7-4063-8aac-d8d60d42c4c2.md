## Highlights
- The new `github-pr` skill has three modes. In review mode, the agent drafts a review of someone else's pull request, and after you approve it, leaves it pending. You name the verdict if you want it submitted.
- In iterate mode, feedback and failing checks on your own pull request are worked through item by item. In adopt mode, a contributor's pull request is fixed and merged under one contract you approve. Nothing is pushed, posted, or merged without your approval.

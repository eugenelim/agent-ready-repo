## Highlights
- You can now review someone else's GitHub pull request: the agent drafts the review, and after you approve it, leaves it pending for you to submit.
- Feedback and failing checks on your own pull request are worked through item by item, and a contributor's pull request can be adopted and merged under one contract you approve. Nothing is pushed, posted, or merged without your approval.

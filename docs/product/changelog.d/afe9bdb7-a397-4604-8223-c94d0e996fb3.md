## Highlights
- The navigate-decisions HTML explorer now looks and works like the navigate-intents view: title-first rows, state words with shapes, and the picked record's detail beside the list.

## Changed
- The `navigate-decisions` export shares the intents view's stylesheet, header and two-zone layout, and fits every width from 320 to 2560 px in light and dark.
- The lineage diagram takes its colours from the shared tokens, so it follows dark mode.
- The page follows the system theme; the Auto / Light / Dark switch, the count tiles and the separate Detail tab are gone, and the snapshot line now carries the record counts.
- The export's content security policy allows styles only by hash and no longer allows inline styles.

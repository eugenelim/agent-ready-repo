## Highlights
- A new guide, Upgrade from a workspace.toml registry, walks adopters through moving every remaining `workspace.toml` entry onto its own artifact with `workspace-status` `repair-plan` and `repair-apply`.
- A fresh `core` install no longer writes `workspace.toml`, and an intent rename runs without one.

## Changed
- `guides/core/how-to/upgrade-from-a-workspace-registry.md` publishes the upgrade notes: finish any recorded `.workspace-migrations.json` operation before upgrading, run `repair-plan` then `repair-apply --yes` (including `needs-to-header`, `captured-entry-drop` and the `refresh-policy-move` to `.work-intake.toml`), the retired `workspace-status` subcommands and their replacements, and the `workspace_status()` MCP changes.
- Guides, pack pages, journeys, skills, manifests and the `core` first-value check route status to `work-loop`'s `dispatch.py` and name `workspace-status` only for the repair. `repo-governance`'s follow-on integration names `work-intake` alone.
- Finding next actions that said "rerun reconciliation" now name `dispatch.py` (dispatch and the MCP tool) or `repair-plan` (`workspace-status`).
- `work-intake`'s intent rename treats an absent `workspace.toml` as a registry it does not touch, and still refuses one that is a link, unreadable, invalid, or present in only some of the commit, the working tree and the index.

## Removed
- The `workspace.toml` seed from `core`, with its `agentbundle` self-host and catalogue-lint entries.
- The `Recover or roll back a migration operation` and `workspace.toml schema` guides; the upgrade notes replace them.

## Highlights
- The offline intent view now answers "How far along is each capability?": every capability under its product strategy, with how much of the work beneath it is delivered, being built or not yet started, and a Browse tree you can narrow to open or done work and regroup by capability.

## Changed
- `navigate-intents`: the exported view's questions are grouped by job — Work, Progress, Tidy and Browse — and gain "How far along is each capability?", which shows delivery-stage counts and a progress bar over all work beneath each capability, grouped under its product strategy, and never a health judgement.
- `navigate-intents`: "Browse all intents" gains Show (all, open, done, keeping parents as marked context rows), Group (full hierarchy or by capability), Expand all and Collapse all.
- `navigate-intents`: the rail links show short labels — "Next up", "Broken", "Capability progress", "Open by capability", "No parent" and "All intents". Each list shows its full question ("What can I work on next?", "What is broken?", "How far along is each capability?", "What is still open under a capability?", "What has no parent?", "Browse all intents") in a line above its title, and each rail link carries that question as its accessible description.
- `navigate-intents`: "All intents" names its sort key — "Under each parent, by name", "Under each capability, by name", or "By name" while a filter is set — and its lead follows the filter and Show choice.

## Fixed
- `navigate-intents`: when capabilities name each other as parent, directly or through other intents, "Capability progress" and Browse's "By capability" now list each of them once instead of dropping them.

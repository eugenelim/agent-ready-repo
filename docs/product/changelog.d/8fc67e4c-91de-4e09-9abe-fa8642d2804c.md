## Highlights
- The offline intent view now answers "How far along is each capability?": every capability under its product strategy, with how much of the work beneath it is delivered, in progress or not started, and a Browse tree you can narrow to open or done work and regroup by capability.

## Changed
- `navigate-intents`: the exported view's questions are grouped by job — Work, Progress, Tidy and Browse — and gain "How far along is each capability?", which shows delivery-stage counts and a progress bar over all work beneath each capability, grouped under its product strategy, and never a health judgement.
- `navigate-intents`: "Browse all intents" gains Show (all, open, done, keeping parents as marked context rows), Group (full hierarchy or by capability), Expand all and Collapse all.

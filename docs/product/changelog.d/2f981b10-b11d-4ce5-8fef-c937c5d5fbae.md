## Highlights
- Requirements ingest now says when it applies: a multi-slice or cross-repository document goes to author-delivery-brief create, a single-feature one to new-spec; shipped skills state the content-not-runtime rule directly instead of citing internal records.

## Changed
- Requirements ingest in `discovery-loop`, its requirements crosswalk, and the run-a-discovery guide now routes a multi-slice or cross-repository BRD, PRD, or SRS to `author-delivery-brief create` and a single-feature document to `new-spec`. The discovery-loop evals gain a single-feature case.
- `discovery-loop`, `discovery-lead`, `explore-options`, `plan-validation`, `frame-domain`, `intent`, `align-value-stream`, and two discovery guides now state the content-not-runtime rule and the three-reviewer ceiling directly instead of citing internal governance records. Meaning is unchanged.

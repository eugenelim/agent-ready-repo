## Highlights
- A spec, brief, intent, or captured item names what it waits for in its own `Depends on:` header, and `workspace-status` blocks on that header instead of a `needs` entry in `workspace.toml`.
- Upgrade with one repair run: `workspace-status repair-plan`, then `repair-apply --yes` on the plan you confirm, moves every typed `needs` edge onto its waiting item and checks that no edge was lost.

## Changed
- `workspace-status` reads `- **Depends on:**` preamble lines of typed references (`spec:<slug>`, `brief:<slug>`, `intent:<slug>` and the other intent kinds) or captured-item paths, and `- **Dependency receipts:**` lines for completion and coordination receipts. A value it cannot resolve gives `invalid_dependency`.
- A `workspace.toml` entry that still carries typed `needs` content gets `unmigrated_needs` and is not dispatchable; its next action names the repair. Legacy string `needs` entries are unchanged.
- `repair-plan` adds `needs-to-header` and `needs-drop-cooled` operations, and `repair-apply` reports the edge sets before and after the write and exits non-zero when they differ.
- `close-work` keeps a completion receipt on the citing item's `Dependency receipts:` line.
- `navigate-intents` recognizes the two new finding codes in its readiness view.

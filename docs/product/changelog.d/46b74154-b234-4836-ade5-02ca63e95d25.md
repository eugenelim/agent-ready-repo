## Highlights
- A spec's `Discovery:` line, which names the intent or research the spec came from, can now name its intent as `outcome:<slug>`, `opportunity:<slug>`, `capability:<slug>`, or `intent:<slug>`. Close-out still finds the same parent intent.
- A spec you add or change is refused before the work loop completes the change when its `Discovery:` points at an intent by file path or link, or names no existing intent of that kind. Research and notes paths stay plain paths and are not checked.
- Older specs you do not touch keep working with their legacy paths.

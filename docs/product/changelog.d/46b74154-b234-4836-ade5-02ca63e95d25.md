## Highlights
- A spec's `Discovery:` can now name an intent by its registered kind, such as `outcome:<slug>` or `capability:<slug>`, and close-out and delivery tracing still find the same parent intent.
- The work-loop finish check and the repository gate now refuse an untyped or broken intent pointer in `Discovery:` for any spec a change adds or modifies; research and notes paths stay plain paths and are not checked.
- New specs are written with the typed form, and older specs you do not touch keep working with their legacy paths.

## Highlights
- work-loop now decides what to start or resume from its own dispatch.py, lists every candidate and asks when several specs are ready, and needs no workspace.toml entry for a spec; the workspace-status spec-entry-drop repair removes the repo-origin spec entries an adopter already has, and navigate-intents export --readiness - now reads work-loop's dispatch output.

## Changed

- `work-loop` Step 0 runs `scripts/dispatch.py` with or without `workspace.toml` and acts on its decision: start, resume, ask with every candidate, or stop with the findings.
- `work-intake`, `new-spec` and the guides no longer register a repo-origin spec; a tracker-origin spec still registers until a later release.
- `workspace-status` retires the `unregistered_work` finding and the Type 1 untracked-spec audit; `repair-plan` gains `spec-entry-drop`, and holds a cooled spec's entry while its lifecycle record lacks the parent the entry declares.
- A `(deferred: <anchor>)` marker also resolves through an open `docs/specs/<anchor>/spec.md`.
- The `navigate-intents` readiness bridge accepts only argless `dispatch.py` output; piping `workspace-status status` now shows readiness as not checked.
- `new-rfc` hands follow-on work to `work-intake` without asking for queue registration.

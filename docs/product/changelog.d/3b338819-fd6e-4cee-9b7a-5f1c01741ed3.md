## Highlights
- The `new-spec` assumptions gate now waits only for items the owner alone can settle: product or preference calls, who-may-approve questions, protected classes, irreversible choices, and items with no stated default.
- Every other open assumption proceeds on a stated, reversible default. It is logged in the spec's Assumptions as a `Defaulted:` line for the owner to read at spec approval, and a default never approves the spec.

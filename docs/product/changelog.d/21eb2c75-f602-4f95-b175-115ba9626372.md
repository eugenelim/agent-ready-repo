## Highlights
- Jira and Jira Align updates now show the exact value each field will hold, let you keep, append to, or replace existing text, and need your approval one record at a time.
- A refused or regretted update can be undone: the old values are saved before every write, and `plan-undo` restores the fields that were written.

## Changed
- The `jira` and `jira-align` skills add `plan-update` (a read-only preview with a digest) and `plan-undo`. `update-issue` and `update` now need `--plan` and `--approved-digest`, and exit 10 without sending a request when the plan does not match, the record changed since the preview, or a Jira Cloud replace would drop tables, panels, links, or other formatting (override with `--allow-lossy`).
- `transition` with field content, `raw` writes, and comment edits are refused. Jira Align `update` sends `PATCH`; `PUT` needs `plan-update --replace-record`.
- Plan and record files are kept under `~/.agentbundle/tracker-write-back/`, owner-only. Retention is manual; the guides say what is safe to delete.
- The story-triage and team-status skills, their evals and examples, and the Atlassian guides use the new flow.

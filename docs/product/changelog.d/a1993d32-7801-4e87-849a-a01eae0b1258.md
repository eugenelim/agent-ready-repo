## Highlights
- Intents, briefs, specs, and captured items can carry an optional Priority field.

## Changed
- Intents, briefs, specs, and captured items can carry a `Priority:` header field with the value urgent, high, medium, or low. An absent field means no priority. The field never decides readiness, dispatch, or order.
- `lint-priority-field.py` checks the field. It runs in `make build-check` and in the work-loop finish checklist.
- The work-loop eval harness gains a case showing that an argless start over two ready specs still picks the first one, whatever their priority.

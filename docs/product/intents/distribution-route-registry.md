# Distribution route registry extraction

- **Status:** Draft
- **Level:** feature

## Outcome

Three real distribution routes use one generic six-field registry without route-specific dispatch branches that the registry subsumes.

## Opportunity

Named route implementations exist without a generic registry once the third route lands.

## Assumptions

- Registry extraction begins only after Phase 1B supplies the third real route implementation.

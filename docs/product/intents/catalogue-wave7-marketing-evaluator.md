# Catalogue marketing evaluator surface

- **Status:** Draft
- **Level:** feature
- **Authority:** [RFC-0076 D10](../../rfc/0076-catalogue-contracts-composition-semantics-discovery.md)

## Outcome

Prospective catalogue evaluators can use a clear marketing surface to understand the catalogue’s open formats, verifiable contracts, ownership model, composition, and release evidence.

## Opportunity

The marketing site does not yet offer an evaluator route or present neutral-index facts and pack relationships in terms that support an informed adoption decision.

## Assumptions

- The technical documentation architecture will provide the evidence destinations linked from the evaluator surface.

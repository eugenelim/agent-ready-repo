Fix: every artifact the handoff's conclusions or its own generator depend on must be carried, so each preflight and blinding claim can be recomputed from the integrated set rather than believed.

**2. The AC-0023 claim that no preliminary outcome entered a later worker prompt is proved by a six-phrase substring search with no positive control.** `.context/experiments/codex-headless-sol-loop-confirmation-r1/tools/t13_blinding.py:60-62`; `.context/codex-headless-sol-loop-confirmation-result.md:114`. A checkpoint's actual content — its learning prose, `36 of 36`, `recall 1.0`, an arm mean, an interval — contains none of those six strings and would pass clean. Nothing anywhere in `tools/` injects a known-bad string to show the scanner fires, so the check has never been observed to fail. `t13_checkpoint.py:9-11` states the payload scan proves the discipline "rather than trusting this note", which is what the instrument cannot do. Fix: the claim must rest on evidence that can fail for the behaviour promised — a demonstrated detection of injected checkpoint content plus a stated argument that no prompt builder reads `checkpoints/` or `analysis/` — or be narrowed to what the six-token search actually establishes.

## Concerns

**3. The frozen severe-defect trigger term is coded as a mean-of-differences test, so it can read zero while arms differ on four of six tasks.** `.context/experiments/codex-headless-sol-loop-confirmation-r1/tools/t13_analysis.py:319-320`; `sealed/t14-trigger-decision.json:24-25`; `.context/codex-headless-sol-loop-confirmation-result.md:248`. The freeze says "any arm differs on residual severe defects at all" (`sealed/design-freeze.json:255`), but the predicate is `mean != 0` per contrast. For separate minus unified the per-task differences are `[-1, 1, -1, 0, 1, 0]` — the arms differ on four subjects — yet the carried figure is `0.0` and the .md reports "separate minus unified +0" as if it showed no difference. Packaging still fires here through the other contrast, so the T13 decision is right by luck; had both contrasts cancelled, the rule would have read "does not fire" while ten per-task differences existed. That is the same substitution-of-the-wrong-statistic failure the sustained blocker names, one layer down, and T14 reuses this predicate. Fix: the coded term must decide the question the freeze asks — whether any arm differs at all — and the reported figure must not present a cancelling mean as evidence of no difference.

**4. The entire sustained blocker turned on which of two metrics a rule names, and the non-authoritative one travels into the durable record as a column of numbers with no reproducible definition. Fix: the carried amendment record must state the adjustment arithmetic and its arm condition precisely enough to recompute the column from the raw grades.

**5. **8. **9. A relaunched process can be lost from the start count, and the cap is enforced on ordinal value rather than process count.** `.context/experiments/codex-headless-sol-loop-confirmation-r1/tools/t13_runner.py:258-272`; `tools/t13_accounting.py:111`; `tools/t13_common.py:170`. On a zero-model-start failure, `quarantine()` renames the reservation and receipt away and `run_one` re-reserves the *same* ordinal, so two CLI processes resolve to one reservation and one receipt; `cli_process_starts` is `len(rows)` over receipts and cannot see the first. The contract counts pre-request failures and zero-output processes as process starts — Run 1's durable total includes its one quarantine — and `reserve` refuses only when `ordinal > 96`, so 96 ordinals plus N relaunches would exceed the cap the refusal is meant to guard (AC-0002). Zero quarantines occurred here, so every reported T13 number is correct; the instrument is what is lossy, and T14 reuses it. Fix: the process-start count and the cap refusal must both be driven by processes launched rather than by ordinals reserved, so no relaunch can pass unrecorded.

**11. **12. The panel's recall result is framed as a lower bound, and the schema-supplied taxonomy that makes 36/36 near-mechanical is not disclosed.** `.context/codex-headless-sol-loop-confirmation-result.md:83,421`; `sealed/schemas/review.schema.json` (`findings.items.invariant` enum, `field_path` enum); `tools/t13_findings.py:41-63`. Crediting requires the reviewer's `invariant` to equal the registry's and its normalised `element_id` to match — and the response schema hands the reviewer the exact seven-value invariant enum and nine-value field enum the oracle scores on. With three controller-authored, structurally decidable defects per subject, recall 1.0 measures selecting the right enum value far more than detection depth. Checkpoints 05 and 09 are honest that detection depth was not tested, which keeps this a framing defect rather than a false conclusion. Fix: state that the reviewer is given the grader's classification vocabulary, and replace the "lower bound" framing with the direction the design supports.

## Nits

**13. The bootstrap resampler has a measurable modulo bias that happens not to move any reported endpoint.** `.context/experiments/codex-headless-sol-loop-confirmation-r1/tools/t13_analysis.py:49`. `vals[next(gen) % n]` draws a single byte, and 256 mod 6 is 4, so indices 0-3 are drawn 43/256 of the time and indices 4-5 42/256; over 600000 draws I measured 100550/100820/100825/100650/98716/98439, a 2.1% underweight on the last two clusters, which are the alphabetically last tasks. Recomputing the three load-bearing contrasts against an unbiased resampler returns the same percentile endpoints to four decimals, so no published interval changes. Fix: make the draw uniform, or record the bias and the check that it does not move the reported endpoints.

**14.

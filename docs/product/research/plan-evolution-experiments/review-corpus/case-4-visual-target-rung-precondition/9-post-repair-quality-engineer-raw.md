## Blockers

**1. `stable_case_key` is not stable across the case lifecycle.** `docs/product/research/plan-evolution-experiments/review-effectiveness-baseline.json:407`. The key includes the stopped revision digest “when available or literal pending,” so the same candidate can hash to one identity at enumeration and another after the normal loop stops, defeating recovery, duplicate/conflict checks, and count/digest reconciliation. Fix: Freeze case identity from one immutable enumeration tuple available at first candidate enumeration, and bind the stopped revision as a separate required row/envelope field that is reconciled without changing identity.

**2. Zero-finding terminal cases have no defined lineage denominator.** `docs/product/research/plan-evolution-experiments/review-effectiveness-baseline.json:471`. The lineage contract defines finding-shaped required fields and a per-case formula, but it does not say how clean normal loops, clean shadow audits, or no-adjudication cases contribute rows and denominators; those cases can be omitted or collapse to all `not_applicable` while still counting toward the cohort. Fix: Freeze the row grain and arithmetic so every terminal admitted case, including zero-finding and no-adjudication paths, contributes a deterministic denominator with reason-coded `not_applicable` fields and cannot be silently dropped.

**3. Cost sufficiency can pass without prose-churn-attributable cost.** `docs/product/research/plan-evolution-experiments/review-effectiveness-baseline.json:511`. The effectiveness gate requires overall normal-loop and study-added cost fields, while the prose-churn-attributable action/token/time fields are only listed later as scorecard outputs, so a future report can satisfy the cost threshold while the core churn-cost measure remains uncomputable. Fix: Make cost sufficiency require attributable review, adjudication, and repair action/token/time lineage for every `prose_churn_flag` event, or force the prospective effectiveness reading to report incomplete when that attribution is unavailable.

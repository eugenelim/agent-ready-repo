## Blockers

**1. The scorecard drops `prose churn` as an outcome.** `docs/specs/plan-evolution-experiments/spec.md:303-320`. The amendment defines a `prose churn event`, but the required scorecard can be complete without reporting prose-churn events or their appeal/adjudication cost, so T14/T15 can pass without measuring the failure mode the amendment was approved to study. Fix: make prose churn a required reported measure with its numerator/denominator and T14/T15 verification mapping, while keeping raw finding count, report length, and `Clean` descriptive only.

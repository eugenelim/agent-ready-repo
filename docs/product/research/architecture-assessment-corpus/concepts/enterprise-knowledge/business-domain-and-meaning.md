# Business domain and meaning — source packet

## Material claims

| Claim | Synthesis | Sources |
| --- | --- | --- |
| BD-1 | Architecture priorities derive from stakeholder concerns, mission drivers, and consequences that repository structure alone cannot establish. | S1, S2, S3 |
| BD-1b | Conflicting, stale, or weakly applicable local context lowers confidence and requires an accountable resolution path. | S1, S2, S3 |

## Sources

- S1: [ISO/IEC/IEEE 42010:2022](https://www.iso.org/standard/74393.html) — stakeholders, concerns, views, decisions, and rationale.
- S2: [NIST CSF 2.0 organizational profiles](https://www.nist.gov/cyberframework/profiles) — organization-specific mission, requirements, risk, and profile tailoring.
- S3: [Azure operating-model readiness](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/cloud-adoption-framework/strategy/prepare-organizational-alignment) — local ownership, alignment, and operating context.

## Counter-evidence and downgrade factors

The portable taxonomy cannot establish an adopter's actual authority, ownership,
inventory currency, standards, operations, or roadmap. One source remains
lower-confidence; implemented evidence can conflict without proving a defect.

## Known unknowns

Applicability and decision authority must be resolved from the adopting organization.

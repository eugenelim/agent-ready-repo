# Design history

Why this delivery's mechanism changed three times before approval. Kept out
of `plan.md`'s Changelog, which the plan template reserves for approvals.
The full review record — 21 raw reports and 4 adjudications across nine
rounds — is under `.context/reviews/`, which is git-ignored, so this file is
the only durable account.

One cause runs through all three collapses: *which cohort facts did this
verdict read?* has no answer a rule can compute. Pinned fields missed two
verdict rows; a guard-derived set missed the `run_id` preflight and the
budget snapshot. The shipped design stops asking and compares the whole
parsed state.

- 2026-09-22 — Polished from round 5 (1 security Blocker, 2 adversarial Blockers, 14 Concerns/Nits). No design change: the mechanism has been stable for two rounds and every finding was specification precision. Notable repairs: AC1 restated as a positional property because no call-graph walk can decide the cohort-read set through indirect dispatch; AC15's membership rule made decidable without naming a function; `ensure_ascii=True` pinned with its reason after a lone-surrogate input was shown to raise inside the hold; the engine-side cohort hold given its own numeric bound; two non-retryable lock classes disclosed; and three test anchors restored to `:1612`, `:4027`, `:4055` after round 4's correction to `:1610/:4025/:4053` and round 5's to `:4026/:4054` were both checked at source and found to point at blank lines.
- 2026-09-22 — Polished from round 6 (1 security Blocker, 2 adversarial Blockers, 4 Concerns; 7 findings, down from 17). No design change. The security Blocker was a real fail-open: `ManagedContentError` subclasses `ValueError` and the reader reports a non-regular file as a *bare* `ValueError`, the same type as the 4300-digit parse failure, so the pinned mapping collapsed unreadable into malformed and made T1's four-distinct assertion unsatisfiable. An `lstat` pre-check now supplies the missing discriminator. AC22's inequality was also inverted: the nested hold must finish *below* the acquisition timeout so contenders do not time out, not above it.
- 2026-09-22 — Polished from round 7 (1 adversarial Blocker, 3 Concerns, 3 Nits; security reported no Blocker and verified all 32 line anchors, the two-vs-seven acquisition arithmetic, and AC22's repaired bound). Two repairs matter. The `lstat` pre-check added in round 6 had itself removed coverage: `_read_managed_bytes` converts its own non-`FileNotFoundError` lstat failures to a bare `ValueError` (`_loop_guards.py:452-457`), so hoisting the call out in front left `EACCES`, `ELOOP`, `ENOTDIR` and `ENAMETOOLONG` with no sentinel and a traceback path out of the hold — the repair had instantiated the class it was written to close. And residual six was under-scoped: the check is `fp0 != fp1`, so *any* sentinel repeated at both samples compares equal, not only two `other-unusable` causes; the same-class case is the larger half and now leads the disclosure, together with the fact that `check_identity` is the sole load-bearing intermediate read because `_budget_snapshot` swallows everything.
- 2026-09-22 — Round 8: security returned `Clean — ready to commit.`; adversarial returned one Concern and no Blocker. The Concern was self-inflicted — round 7 added "that section is the count's single home" while five sites still carried the literal number, so the claim contradicted itself. The count now appears only at the Assumptions heading and every other site refers to the section. Security's clean was not re-run for this edit: it removes a numeral from five sentences of working-material prose and changes no criterion, no residual's content and no security property, so it cannot invalidate that verdict.

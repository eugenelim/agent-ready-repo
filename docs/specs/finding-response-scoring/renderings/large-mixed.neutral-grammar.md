# Findings: Large mixed (neutral-grammar)

- F1 — The spec's `Constrained by: none` claim is false because AC3 contradicts the accepted RFC-0077 ancestor-only scope-matching contract. Required outcome and constraints: the spec accurately identifies RFC-0077 as governing authority, and its scope-matching contract remains ancestor-only.
- F2 — AC4 falsely claims nested result sets: its query-independent sort followed by top-12 truncation allows a subset's top 12 to contain an entry outside its superset's top 12, although AC4's operative matching clause remains true. Required outcome and constraints: the contract guarantees that pre-truncation match counts are non-increasing as a query narrows, without asserting that truncated returned sets are nested.
- F3 — `_scope_matches` has an uncovered second caller in `_pending_from_loaded_partitions`, where changing matching semantics would also affect journal-capacity processing and couple modes governed as isolated by ADR-0082. Required outcome and constraints: enquiry matching can change without changing journal-capacity matching, the pending path retains its existing behavior, and both call paths are independently covered.
- F4 — The version-bump acceptance criterion omits the changelog entry required in the same pull request by repository conventions, RFC-0095, and `packs/AGENTS.local.md`. Required outcome and constraints: a version bump is incomplete unless its required changelog entry is present in the same pull request under repository conventions, RFC-0095, and `packs/AGENTS.local.md`.
- F5 — Plan task T4's `atoms(pre)` versus `atoms(split(pre))` assertion cannot detect a wrong splitter because both sides use the same splitter, and its set comparison hides a deduplication the sanctioned writer would refuse. Required outcome and constraints: the migration is demonstrably faithful to the original atom order without relying on the splitter under test, and duplicate input is demonstrably refused under sanctioned writer semantics.
- F6 — AC9 specifies a before-and-after process step but plan task T4 names no artifact location for its record. Required outcome and constraints: the required before-and-after record has one exact repository location established before execution.
- F7 — The plan leaves the `.` atom's specificity undefined; its stated base reduction makes `.` specificity 1 and ranks `.`-scoped topics above every empty-base glob, contrary to the Objective. Required outcome and constraints: no scope expression outranks another purely as a side effect of how its base is reduced, and the ranking the Objective states holds for every scope form the committed corpus actually contains.
- F8 — Plan task T4 bypasses the sanctioned `write_topic` path, including scope validation and the writer lock, even though RFC-0077 requires that path. Required outcome and constraints: every topic change satisfies RFC-0077's sanctioned-write invariant, including scope validation and the writer lock.
- F9 — The proposed migration blesses glob syntax while repairing comma-joined scopes without giving a rationale, although RFC-0077's scope grammar admits neither form. Required outcome and constraints: no migrated scope depends on syntax outside RFC-0077's grammar, and every prior comma-joined or glob scope has a recorded derivation to valid repository-relative path atoms.
- F10 — The spec and plan duplicate load-bearing facts, including the version-bump conclusion, the count of 30 topics, and the `enquiry_bodies=12` limit, creating repair drift across multiple sites. Required outcome and constraints: the spec and plan expose one unambiguous authoritative value for the version-bump conclusion, the count of 30 topics, the `enquiry_bodies=12` limit, and every other load-bearing fact, without duplicated normative statements that can drift.
- F11 — The spec retcons earlier work as though it had already occurred at `spec.md:27`, `spec.md:90-92`, and `spec.md:119`. Required outcome and constraints: the delivery record distinguishes proposed work from present baseline evidence and makes no claim that proposed work has already occurred.
- F12 — The Objective's success statement that enquiry would find what a maintainer would have found by grepping by hand has no observable post-condition or derivable test. Required outcome and constraints: success is observable as every active topic matching at least one concrete repository-path query through the production matcher.
- F13 — AC6 leaks the plan-owned mechanism phrase `reduces to an empty base` into the contract instead of using the spec's own `corpus-wide` term. Required outcome and constraints: the root-scope case is expressed consistently with the contract's corpus-wide concept and does not expose plan-owned base-reduction mechanics.
- F14 — Plan task T7 cites AC10 and AC11, but its actual acceptance criteria are AC11 and AC12. Required outcome and constraints: pending-path non-regression work is traceable to both the unchanged pending-selection behavior and the full project-knowledge test-suite obligation.
- F15 — Plan tasks T2, T5, and T6 declare no verification mode, and T6's behavior has no Testing Strategy row from which to inherit one. Required outcome and constraints: the verification mode is unambiguous for enquiry matching, specificity ranking, and enquiry-exclusion work, and the testing strategy governs exclusion behavior.

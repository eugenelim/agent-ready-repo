## Why arm D exists and why no judgment used it

Arm D was the unrouted half of a planned cross-family pair: a Codex baseline
against a Codex routed arm, to test whether the overlay helps an author from a
different model family. Its routed partner was started and then deliberately
stopped once the control pair returned, because a single cross-family pair would
inherit the same defect the control had just exposed — one pair cannot separate
an overlay from that author's own run-to-run spread, and answering it for Codex
would have needed a Codex control too.

Arm D is kept rather than deleted because it is real evidence of something else:
the same procedure and brief, run by a different model family, produced a
document of 3275 words against arm A's 3854 — 2728 against 3300 by the gate's
own count. That is the cross-family baseline spread on the one dimension this
record can re-derive, and a later measurement study starts from it rather than
re-running it. The two runs also reported loading different concept sets, but
no committed artifact records either list, so no count is claimed here.

What changed in the audit log

Product notes — six minute read

The audit log now records the actor, the workspace, and the object for every write, and keeps them for thirteen months.

Why the retention changed

Thirteen months covers a full year plus the month teams usually spend closing it out, which is when most people go looking.

Older entries are removed on a rolling basis rather than in one sweep, so the log stays a predictable size.

What you need to do

Nothing. Existing exports keep working, and the new fields appear alongside the ones already there.

If you parse the export, treat unknown fields as optional — more will be added.

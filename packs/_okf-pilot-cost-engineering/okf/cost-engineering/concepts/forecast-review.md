# Forecast review

Use this concept when a maintainer needs to explain a forward-looking cost
estimate from a generic usage assumption. Keep the forecast visibly tied to its
assumptions and avoid presenting it as a guarantee.

Every forecast note should name the baseline period, growth assumption,
excluded costs, and the owner who can revise the estimate when usage changes.

# Unit economics review

Use this concept when a maintainer needs to compare request volume, unit price,
and billed usage for a generic AI-enabled workflow. Keep the calculation as a
review aid and do not infer permission to change budgets, vendors, or account
settings.

## Signals

- Measured requests and tokens for the same time window.
- A declared unit price or placeholder rate supplied by the operator.
- A named owner who can confirm whether the workload pattern is expected.

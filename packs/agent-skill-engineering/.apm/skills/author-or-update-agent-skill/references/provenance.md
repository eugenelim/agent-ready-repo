# Provenance

Every claim a provider serves carries where it came from and when it was last
checked.

Record, per claim group, the basis it rests on and that basis's evidence. A claim
observed in practice records the observations that support it and the population
they were drawn from, and states plainly that it is not established beyond that
population. A claim resting on a published contract records the clause, the
runtimes that document that clause, and for every source its identity, when it
was retrieved, and its version state.

Record a revalidation trigger: the event after which the claim must be checked
again. A claim with no trigger silently becomes folklore.

Name the person who judged the evidence sufficient. Form can be checked
mechanically; whether evidence actually supports a claim is a judgement, and a
judgement with no name attached cannot be revisited.

Never let a generated projection be the source. Edit the authored bundle and
regenerate, so provenance survives the next compile.

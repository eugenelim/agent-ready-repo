# Declared unpopulated leaves

Every taxonomy leaf this corpus does not carry, with why it is absent and
what would admit it. A leaf appears here or in the topic set, never in
both and never in neither. This register is not itself a topic and answers
no request; it records the shape of what is missing so a reader can tell an
unevidenced subject from an overlooked one.

## inline-and-progressive-reference-skills

**Why absent.** Authored, then withdrawn: measured retrieval could not
separate it from instruction density and progressive disclosure. Three natural
phrasings of its own subject each selected both.

**What would admit it.** A body whose subject is not already named in another
topic's routing signals, selected alone by at least two measured prompts.

## multimode-artifact-and-script-backed-skills

**Why absent.** The census does record skills that are both artifact-producing
and script-backed, in more than one pack — but none of them also declares
multiple user-visible modes, so the three-way pattern this leaf names is
observed nowhere and no promotion class is evidenced.

**What would admit it.** Two packs whose skills are artifact-producing,
script-backed, and multi-mode at once — the dimension the observed pairs lack.

## routers-composed-workflows-and-lifecycle-skills

**Why absent.** The census does record composed workflows that also carry a
state or lifecycle model, across several packs — but none of them routes or
dispatches, so the router dimension this leaf names is observed nowhere and no
promotion class is evidenced.

**What would admit it.** Two packs whose composed workflows also dispatch to
sub-workflows — the routing dimension the observed pairs lack.

## compatibility-and-runtime-package-patterns

**Why absent.** No body can be written without advertising a packaging mode
this corpus does not offer, which its own boundaries forbid.

**What would admit it.** The runtime profiles that make packaging claims
verifiable, after which the mode's guidance can be stated without advertising
it.

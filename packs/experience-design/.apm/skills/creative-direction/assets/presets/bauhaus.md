# Aesthetic direction: Bauhaus

<!-- A preset, not a finished direction. It supplies the PRECEDENT referent.
     Ground every goal in its persona, standards and platform conventions, and
     run the counterfactual check, before this is a direction. -->

## Formal commitments

- Build from function-led construction, simple balanced geometry, and a limited set of geometric primitives.
- Integrate typography with the composition rather than laying it on top.
- Apply applied-art and industrial-production logic to repeated parts and their relationships.
- Treat colour and spatial composition as formal studies.
- The popular primary-colours-plus-circle-square-triangle shorthand is recognisable, but it is not a rule.

## What a direction borrowing this leaves behind

It leaves behind decoration without a compositional job, arbitrary geometry, type detached from construction, and historical shorthand treated as a mandatory recipe.

## Best used for:

Function-led cultural, educational, or product surfaces where composition itself explains the system.

# Aesthetic direction: Swiss / International Typographic

<!-- A preset, not a finished direction. It supplies the PRECEDENT referent.
     Ground every goal in its persona, standards and platform conventions, and
     run the counterfactual check, before this is a direction. -->

## Formal commitments

- Use an explicit typographic grid to organise an asymmetric composition.
- Set sans-serif type flush left with a ragged right edge.
- Prefer photography to illustration, with near-zero ornament and restrained colour.
- Keep the composition rational and unornamented; hierarchy comes from type, alignment, and space.

## What a direction borrowing this leaves behind

It leaves behind decorative storytelling, centred symmetry, illustrative substitution, and type choices that compete with the information order.

## Best used for:

Information-rich public surfaces that need clarity, order, and a direct editorial voice.

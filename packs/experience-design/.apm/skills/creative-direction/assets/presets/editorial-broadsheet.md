# Aesthetic direction: Editorial broadsheet

<!-- A preset, not a finished direction. It supplies the PRECEDENT referent.
     Ground every goal in its persona, standards and platform conventions, and
     run the counterfactual check, before this is a direction. -->

## Formal commitments

- Use a rigid multi-column grid of five or six columns and modular story units.
- Make strong type hierarchy, headlines, decks, and bylines the primary navigation system.
- Use rules and dividers as structural devices, not decoration.
- Maintain disciplined white space and explicit image-and-caption relationships.

## What a direction borrowing this leaves behind

It leaves behind a feed-like stream, undifferentiated story weight, borderless grouping, and images that lack editorial context.

## Best used for:

Publication surfaces where readers must scan, rank, and move among many related stories.

## Direction sheet

| Axis | This direction commits to |
| --- | --- |
| Grid grammar | `[column]` `[rigid]` A five- or six-column grid holds modular story units; transformations retain story order and column logic. |
| Alignment and equilibrium | `[baseline]` `[symmetric]` Shared text lines and column edges establish a stable field across the page. |
| Spatial density | `[dense]` Each screenful supports several distinct stories while preserving their rank. |
| Whitespace distribution | `[even]` Disciplined margins, gutters, section gaps, and micro spacing separate stories without wasting the field. |
| Hierarchy and scale contrast | `[steep]` Lead stories dominate; headings, decks, and bylines mark progressively smaller roles. |
| Containment and boundary strength | `[ruled]` Rules and dividers define story boundaries; overlap is not permitted. |
| Section and scroll rhythm | `[episodic]` `[regular]` Repeated story units create regular pacing through a long page. |
| Type voice | `[serif]` A newspaper-like serif voice gives headlines, decks, bylines, and body copy a shared editorial character. |
| Type hierarchy | `[dramatic]` Headline, deck, byline, caption, and body roles have unmistakable relationships. |
| Chromatic intensity | `[restrained]` Accent is spent sparingly so story rank and structure remain primary. |
| Form | `[rectilinear]` Straight rules and column boundaries make the editorial system legible. |
| Material and depth | `[flat]` Story relationships are communicated through hierarchy and rules, not depth. |
| Ornament and texture | `[none]` Structure, rather than texture, carries the editorial character. |
| Image treatment | `[photographic]` Images have a clear story role and remain paired with captions. |
| Motion character | `[still]` Changes remain discrete so scanning and reading retain priority. |

# explore — generating candidates for the audit

> **Loaded when:** `explore` is the active operation.
> **Question:** what materially different directions are available for this brief?

## Before you derive

Name two arrangements before drawing any referents:

- **The category default** — the arrangement that the audience would expect
  from any product in this space. Name it specifically: describe the layout
  grammar, type voice, chromatic character, and material quality that together
  define what "normal" looks like here.
- **Its predictable opposite** — the direction that reacts against the default
  symmetrically. When the default is dense and structured, the predictable
  opposite is sparse and expressive; when the default is monochromatic, its
  opposite saturates.

Exclude both from the candidate set. The default is already occupied. Its
predictable opposite is a reflex, not an idea.

## Deriving referents from the audience's world

Candidates come from what the audience encounters — not from what software
looks like, but from the objects, documents, and spaces they use in their own
practice. Other software products are not the primary source. A candidate
surface drawn mainly from what applications look like has entered the visual
conversation the category already holds, not a new one.

Draw referents from these source classes. Each is a direction to look, not a
closed category — follow the audience's world wherever it leads:

- **Publications** — journals, magazines, newspapers, specialist reports, and
  working manuals that the audience reads professionally or recreationally
- **Physical artifacts** — equipment, instruments, materials, and objects from
  the audience's domain
- **Instruments and notation** — sheet music, technical drawings, notation
  systems, and other structured marks the audience produces or reads
- **Maps** — cartographic, schematic, and diagrammatic traditions the audience
  navigates by
- **Architecture** — the built environments and spatial grammar the audience
  occupies or moves through
- **Packaging** — the goods the audience buys, uses, or produces, and the
  labelling conventions that surround them
- **Signage** — wayfinding and regulatory systems from the audience's
  environment
- **Historical graphic systems** — visual traditions predating the digital era
  that the audience's field carries forward

### The software-product test

A candidate derived primarily from
a named software product has not left the category default. Even a widely
admired product from a different category is already a designed surface — it
carries someone else's direction, not a referent drawn from the audience's own
world. Software products may appear as secondary references alongside domain
referents; they may not anchor a candidate on their own.

### Material-family spread

After collecting candidate referents, count how many share one material family
— print, textile, architectural, industrial, cartographic, and so on.
When more than half the candidate referents share one material family, the
derivation stopped at the obvious artifact and must continue. Return to the
source classes above and derive from a family the set does not yet hold.

## Constructing candidates

For each candidate direction, record the referents whose combined visual logic
produces it. For each referent, name the specific qualities you are taking and
the qualities you are leaving — a referent taken whole is a copy, not a
direction.

Translate the kept qualities into axis tokens.
Each surviving candidate carries its own filled direction sheet, covering all
fifteen axes. Candidates differ by axis tokens rather than by adjective: a cell
that reads "bold and structured" rather than a token from the vocabulary is not
a filled cell. Mark a cell `[platform-default]` only when the platform
genuinely owns that decision.

## Candidate sheets stay in the session

Candidate sheets are held in the session and are not written to disk. The
`converge` operation is the only operation that writes a file. This skill
declares exactly one write target, and candidate sheets committed to disk would
create a second. A tool that scans the direction folder on `type:` alone would
then count rejected candidates as standing directions.

## Handing off to the audit

Pass the candidate set to `references/divergence-audit.md`. A set failing the
six-of-fifteen minimum pairwise distance is not a candidate set — it is a
collection of near-duplicates. The author returns here, varies the material
family or the source class, and derives more candidates until the set clears
the threshold.

The audit certifies that the set is worth presenting. Selecting among
candidates belongs to `converge`.
